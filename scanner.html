Recognized stickers

Tap any non-center sticker to cycle through colors and correct a misread. Every color should appear exactly nine times.

This checks sticker colors and counts only. It does not verify every corner/edge permutation or generate a solution.

Before following the solution: white center on top, green center facing you, red center on your right. Keep the cube in this orientation while doing the moves.
